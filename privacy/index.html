Privacy Policy

Last updated September 24, 2026.

The short version

FaceOffFood is a hobby recipe site with games and an optional account. If you don't sign in, nothing about you leaves your device. If you sign in, we store your email, your XP and game progress, and any ratings you leave, so we can show them back to you across devices. We don't sell your data, and we don't run ads or third-party trackers on this site today. If that changes, this page changes with it.

Playing without an account

You can spin the wheel, run food face-offs, take the quiz, roll the dice and use Fridge Raid without signing in. Your XP, cooked recipes, badges and fridge selections are saved only in your browser's local storage on your own device. We never see this information, and it disappears if you clear your browser data or switch devices.

If you create an account

Signing in lets your progress follow you between devices. Creating an account collects:

  • Your email address and password, used only to sign you in. Your password is never visible to us; it is handled by our authentication provider, Supabase, in encrypted form.
  • Your game progress — XP, level, cooked recipes, badges and play counts — so it can sync across your devices.
  • Recipe ratings you leave (1 to 5 stars). Your star ratings are tied to your account, but other players only ever see the average and total count, never your individual rating or identity.

We use your email for account sign-in and, if you request it, a password-reset link. We do not send marketing email and do not share your email with anyone else.

Who processes this data

FaceOffFood is a small, independently run site. We use a short list of outside services to run it, and each only sees what it needs to do its job:

  • Supabase hosts our database and handles sign-in. Your account data, progress and ratings live there.
  • Brevo sends account emails (sign-up confirmation, password reset) on our behalf. It sees your email address only for that purpose.
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare host and deliver the website itself, and see standard technical logs (like your IP address and browser type) the way any website host does.
  • Pexels supplies some recipe photos. Browsing the site does not send Pexels any information about you.

None of these companies are permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your profile, progress and ratings from our database.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time from the menu in the top right of the site. To have your account and its data deleted, email us at the address below with the email you signed up with, and we'll remove it. To stop syncing altogether, just don't sign in — the games work fully without an account.

Children

FaceOffFood is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under that age. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If what we collect or how we use it changes — for example, if we add analytics or advertising in the future — we'll update this page and change the date at the top.

Red Curry with Sweet Potato

30 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Simmering the sweet potato in the coconut milk lets its starch thicken the sauce naturally.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Fry the curry paste in a spoonful of the coconut milk for 1 minute.
  2. Add the rest of the milk, sweet potatoes and 1 cup water and simmer 12 minutes.
  3. Add the pepper for 3 minutes. Finish with soy sauce, lime juice and basil and serve over rice.

Classic Greek Salad

10 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Cutting the vegetables into large chunks and dressing just before serving keeps everything crisp.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Cut the tomatoes, cucumber and onion into large chunks and combine with the olives.
  2. Whisk oil, vinegar and oregano and dress the salad.
  3. Top with a slab of feta and a pinch more oregano.

Shrimp Saganaki

25 minSeafoodContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Feta melts into the tomato sauce, and the shrimp finish cooking in it in just a few minutes.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Soften the onion and garlic in olive oil, add the tomatoes and simmer 10 minutes.
  2. Add the shrimp and cook 3 minutes until pink.
  3. Crumble the feta over the top, warm until soft, and finish with parsley.

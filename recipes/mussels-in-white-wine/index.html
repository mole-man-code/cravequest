Mussels in White Wine

25 minSeafoodContains meat or fishEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Covering the pot lets the mussels steam open in their own liquor, and they are done the moment they open.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Soften the shallot and garlic in butter, add the wine, and bring to a boil.
  2. Add the mussels, cover, and steam 5 to 6 minutes, shaking the pot. Discard any that stay closed.
  3. Finish with parsley and serve with bread for the broth.

Stovetop Mac and Cheese

30 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Grating the cheese yourself and thickening the milk with a little flour keeps the sauce smooth instead of grainy.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Cook the macaroni until just tender, drain.
  2. Melt the butter, whisk in the flour and mustard for 1 minute, then slowly whisk in the milk until thick.
  3. Off the heat, stir in the cheese until smooth, then fold in the pasta.

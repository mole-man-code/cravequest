Street Corn Salad

15 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Charring the corn in a hot dry pan gives the smoky flavor of grilled street corn with no grill.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Char the corn in a hot dry skillet for 5 to 6 minutes, stirring rarely.
  2. Toss with the mayonnaise, lime juice and chili powder while warm.
  3. Fold in the cotija and cilantro and serve warm or cold.

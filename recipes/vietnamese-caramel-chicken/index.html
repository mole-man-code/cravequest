Vietnamese Caramel Chicken

40 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Caramelizing the sugar until amber before adding the fish sauce gives the chicken a deep, sticky sweet-salty glaze.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Cook the sugar in a pot with 2 tbsp water until amber. Add the shallot, garlic and ginger for 30 seconds.
  2. Add the chicken, fish sauce and 1/2 cup water; simmer, turning, for 25 minutes until the sauce is thick and glossy.
  3. Top with scallions and serve with rice.

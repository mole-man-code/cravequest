Coconut Mushroom Tom Kha

30 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Bruising the lemongrass and simmering gently releases its aroma without splitting the coconut milk.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Smash the lemongrass, then simmer it with the ginger and stock for 10 minutes.
  2. Add the mushrooms and coconut milk; keep at a gentle simmer for 8 minutes.
  3. Season with soy sauce and lime juice, then top with chilies and cilantro.

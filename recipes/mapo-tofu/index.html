Mapo Tofu

30 minTofuVegetarianEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Frying the chile paste until the oil turns red builds the sauce's deep flavor before the tofu goes in.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Fry the paste and pork until the oil turns red.
  2. Add stock and tofu cubes, simmer 5 minutes.
  3. Thicken with slurry, top with peppercorns and scallions.

Gyudon

20 minBeef / PorkContains meat or fishEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Thin-sliced beef needs only a minute in the simmering sauce, so the whole bowl comes together in about 20 minutes.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Simmer the dashi, soy sauce, mirin and sugar. Add the onion and cook 5 minutes until soft.
  2. Add the beef and cook just until no longer pink.
  3. Spoon over rice with some of the sauce and top with pickled ginger.

Weeknight Chicken Ramen

35 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Simmering miso and soy into stock at the end, not at a boil, keeps the flavor rounded and savory.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Sear the chicken until browned and cooked through; slice.
  2. Simmer the stock with ginger and garlic for 10 minutes. Whisk the miso and soy into a ladle of broth, then stir back in.
  3. Cook the noodles separately, divide into bowls, ladle over broth and top with chicken, egg and scallions.

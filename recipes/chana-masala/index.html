Chana Masala

35 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Blooming the spices in oil and mashing a few chickpeas builds a thick, deeply flavored sauce from pantry staples.

Method

  1. Brown the onion, add ginger, garlic and spices for 1 minute.
  2. Add tomatoes and chickpeas, simmer 20 minutes.
  3. Mash a few chickpeas to thicken; finish with cilantro.

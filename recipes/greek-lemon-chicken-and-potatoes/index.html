Greek Lemon Chicken and Potatoes

55 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Roasting the potatoes in the chicken's lemony juices lets them absorb flavor as they brown.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 210°C. Toss the chicken and potatoes with lemon juice, oil, garlic, oregano and salt.
  2. Add the stock to the pan and roast 30 minutes.
  3. Turn everything and roast 20 minutes more until the chicken is deep golden and the potatoes are tender.

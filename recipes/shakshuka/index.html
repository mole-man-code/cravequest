Shakshuka

25 minEggVegetarianEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Cooking the tomatoes down first gives the eggs a thick sauce to nestle in, so the whites set before the yolks harden.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Soften onion and pepper with the spices.
  2. Add tomatoes, simmer 10 minutes.
  3. Crack in eggs, cover, cook until whites set.

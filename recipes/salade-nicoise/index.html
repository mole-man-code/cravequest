Salade Niçoise

35 minSeafoodContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Cooking the potatoes and green beans in the same pot at different times saves a pan, and dressing the warm potatoes lets them absorb the vinaigrette.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes 12 minutes, adding the green beans for the last 3. Boil the eggs 8 minutes and peel.
  2. Whisk the Dijon, vinegar and olive oil. Toss the warm potatoes and beans with half of it.
  3. Arrange with the tuna, tomatoes, olives and halved eggs, and drizzle with the rest.

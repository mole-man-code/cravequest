Vietnamese Chicken Salad

25 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Shredding the cabbage very fine and dressing it just before serving keeps the salad crunchy and bright.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Whisk the lime juice, fish sauce and sugar until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Toss the cabbage, carrot, chicken and mint with the dressing.
  3. Top with peanuts and serve right away.

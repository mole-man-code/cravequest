Sheet-Pan Sausage and Veg

35 minBeef / PorkContains meat or fishEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Cutting everything to a similar size and roasting hot means one pan and one timer.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Chop everything into bite-size pieces, toss in oil.
  2. Roast at 220°C for 25 minutes, flipping once.
  3. Drizzle with mustard.

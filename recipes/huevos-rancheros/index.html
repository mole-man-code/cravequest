Huevos Rancheros

25 minEggVegetarianEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Warming the tortillas and frying the eggs in the same skillet keeps this to one pan and one quick sauce.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Warm the beans in a small pot and the salsa in another.
  2. Toast the tortillas in an oiled skillet until pliable; set aside. Fry the eggs in the same pan.
  3. Layer beans, an egg and salsa on each tortilla; top with avocado and cheese.

Tofu Vermicelli Bowls

30 minTofuVegetarianEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Pan-frying the tofu until golden gives it a crisp edge that stands up to the dressing.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Pan-fry the tofu in oil until golden on all sides.
  2. Whisk soy sauce, lime juice, sugar and a splash of water for the dressing.
  3. Layer noodles, greens, herbs and vegetables in bowls; top with tofu, peanuts and dressing.

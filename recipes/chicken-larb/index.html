Chicken Larb

25 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Cooking the chicken gently in a little water keeps it tender and crumbly, and the lime and herbs go in at the end so they stay bright.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Cook the chicken in a splash of water, breaking it up, until just done. Cool slightly.
  2. Toss with lime juice, fish sauce, shallot and chilies.
  3. Fold in the herbs and serve in lettuce leaves with rice.

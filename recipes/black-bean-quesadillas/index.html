Black Bean Quesadillas

15 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Mashing the beans makes a spreadable filling that holds together instead of sliding out.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Mash beans with cumin and a pinch of salt.
  2. Fill tortillas with beans and cheese.
  3. Griddle 2 minutes per side until crisp; serve with salsa.

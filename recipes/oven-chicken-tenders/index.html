Oven Chicken Tenders

35 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Toasting the panko in a little oil first means the tenders brown in the oven without deep frying.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Toss the panko with 2 tbsp oil and toast in a dry pan until golden. Heat the oven to 220°C.
  2. Coat the chicken in flour, then eggs whisked with mustard, then panko.
  3. Bake on a rack over a sheet pan for 15 to 18 minutes until crisp and cooked through.

Beef and Broccoli

25 minBeef / PorkContains meat or fishEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Slicing the steak thinly against the grain and tossing it in cornstarch keeps it tender and helps the sauce cling.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Toss the beef with cornstarch and a splash of soy sauce. Whisk the remaining soy, oyster sauce and 1/4 cup water.
  2. Sear the beef in a very hot pan in two batches; remove. Stir-fry the broccoli with a splash of water until bright and tender.
  3. Add ginger and garlic, return the beef, pour in the sauce and toss until glossy.

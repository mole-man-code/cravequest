French Onion Soup

60 minVegetablesContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Cooking the onions slowly until deeply browned is the whole flavor of the soup, and a splash of wine at the end lifts the sweetness.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Cook the onions in butter over medium-low heat for 35 to 40 minutes, stirring, until deep brown.
  2. Add the wine and reduce, then add the stock and thyme and simmer 15 minutes.
  3. Ladle into oven-safe bowls, top with baguette and Gruyère, and broil until bubbling and browned.

Shrimp Pad Thai

30 minSeafoodContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Soaking the noodles in warm rather than boiling water keeps them from going gummy when they finish cooking in the wok.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Soak the noodles in warm water until pliable. Whisk the tamarind, fish sauce and sugar.
  2. Cook the shrimp in a hot wok until pink and push aside. Scramble the eggs.
  3. Add the noodles and sauce and toss until absorbed. Add sprouts and scallions and top with peanuts and lime.

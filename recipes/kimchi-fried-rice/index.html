Kimchi Fried Rice

20 minEggContains meat or fishEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Frying the kimchi first concentrates its tang and heat before the rice goes in.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Fry the kimchi in oil for 3 minutes until slightly caramelized.
  2. Add the rice and gochujang and stir-fry until the grains are hot and coated.
  3. Finish with sesame oil and scallions and top with fried eggs.

Caramelized Shrimp

20 minSeafoodContains meat or fishEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

A short, hot simmer reduces the sauce to a sticky glaze before the shrimp have a chance to toughen.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Cook the sugar with 1 tbsp water until deep amber. Add the shallot and garlic for 30 seconds.
  2. Add the shrimp and fish sauce and cook, stirring, 3 to 4 minutes until the sauce clings and the shrimp are pink.
  3. Finish with lots of black pepper and scallions and serve with rice.

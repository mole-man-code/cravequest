Chicken Parmesan

45 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Pounding the cutlets thin lets them cook through in the time it takes the cheese to melt and brown.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Dip the cutlets in beaten egg, then a mix of panko and Parmesan.
  2. Pan-fry in olive oil 3 minutes per side until golden.
  3. Top with tomatoes and mozzarella and broil 4 minutes until melted. Finish with basil.

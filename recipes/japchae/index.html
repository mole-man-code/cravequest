Japchae

40 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Cooking each vegetable separately keeps their colors and textures distinct, and dressing the noodles while warm lets them absorb the sauce.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Boil the noodles 6 minutes until chewy and tender, drain, and cut them a few times with scissors.
  2. Stir-fry the onion, carrot and mushrooms separately in a little oil; blanch the spinach and squeeze dry.
  3. Toss everything with the soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil and garlic while still warm.

Crispy Fish Tacos

30 minSeafoodContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

A cornstarch and flour coating browns fast in a shallow pan, and a cool cabbage slaw balances the richness.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Toss the cabbage with lime juice, yogurt and salt for the slaw.
  2. Cut the fish into strips, season, and coat in flour and cornstarch.
  3. Shallow-fry 2 to 3 minutes per side until crisp and serve in warm tortillas with slaw and cilantro.

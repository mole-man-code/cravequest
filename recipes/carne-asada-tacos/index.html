Carne Asada Tacos

30 minBeef / PorkContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

A short citrus marinade tenderizes flank steak without turning it mushy, and slicing against the grain keeps every bite tender.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Marinate the steak in citrus juice, garlic, cumin and salt for at least 15 minutes.
  2. Sear in a very hot pan 4 minutes per side. Rest 5 minutes.
  3. Slice thinly against the grain and serve in warm tortillas with onion and cilantro.

Dry-Fried Green Beans

20 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Cooking the beans in a mostly dry pan until they wrinkle concentrates their flavor and gives them a chewy, blistered skin.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Fry the beans in a little oil over high heat, turning, until blistered and wrinkled, about 6 minutes.
  2. Push aside and cook the garlic, ginger and chilies for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the soy sauce and sugar and toss until the beans are coated.

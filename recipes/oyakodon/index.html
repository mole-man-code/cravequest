Oyakodon

25 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Adding the egg in two stages leaves part of it fully set and part silky, which is the whole point of the dish.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Simmer dashi, soy, mirin and sugar; add the onion and chicken and cook 6 minutes.
  2. Pour in two-thirds of the egg, cover for 1 minute, then add the rest.
  3. Slide over rice while the egg is still soft and top with scallions.

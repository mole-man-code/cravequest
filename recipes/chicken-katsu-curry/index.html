Chicken Katsu Curry

40 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 3 of 3

Why this recipe works

Pounding the chicken thin means it cooks in the time it takes the panko to turn golden.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Simmer carrot and onion in water, melt in roux.
  2. Bread the pounded chicken: flour, egg, panko.
  3. Shallow-fry 3 minutes per side; slice over rice and ladle sauce.

Kung Pao Chicken

30 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Coating the chicken in cornstarch keeps it juicy over high heat, and toasting the chilies first perfumes the oil.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with cornstarch and a splash of soy sauce. Mix the remaining soy, vinegar and sugar.
  2. Toast the chilies in hot oil for 15 seconds, then stir-fry the chicken until browned.
  3. Add ginger, garlic, scallions and the sauce; toss with the peanuts.

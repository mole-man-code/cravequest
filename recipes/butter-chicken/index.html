Butter Chicken

Why this recipe works

A yogurt marinade tenderizes the chicken, and charring it adds the smokiness a tandoor would.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Marinate chicken in yogurt and spices, ideally an hour.
  2. Broil or sear until charred.
  3. Simmer tomato, butter and spices 15 minutes; add cream and chicken.

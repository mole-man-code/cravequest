Chicken Souvlaki

35 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

A marinade of lemon, oregano and olive oil tenderizes the chicken in as little as 20 minutes.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken in lemon juice, oil, garlic and oregano for at least 20 minutes.
  2. Thread onto skewers and grill or sear 10 minutes, turning, until charred.
  3. Serve in pita with tzatziki, tomato and onion.

French Omelette

10 minEggVegetarianEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Constant stirring over medium heat makes small, soft curds, and rolling the omelette out of the pan keeps the surface pale and tender.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Beat the eggs with salt until no streaks remain.
  2. Melt the butter until foaming, add the eggs, and stir with a spatula while shaking the pan until creamy curds form.
  3. Tilt the pan, roll the omelette to the edge, and turn out seam side down. Top with parsley.

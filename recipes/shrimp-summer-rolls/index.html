Shrimp Summer Rolls

30 minSeafoodContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Soaking rice paper for only a few seconds lets it finish softening as you roll, so it does not tear.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Cook the vermicelli, rinse, and prep every filling within reach.
  2. Dip a wrapper in warm water for 5 seconds and lay it flat. Add shrimp, vermicelli, herbs and vegetables.
  3. Fold in the sides and roll tightly. Serve with peanut sauce.

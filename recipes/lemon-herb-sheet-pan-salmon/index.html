Lemon Herb Sheet-Pan Salmon

25 minSeafoodContains meat or fishEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Roasting the salmon on a bed of lemon slices protects the underside from drying out and perfumes the fish.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Toss the zucchini and tomatoes with oil, garlic and oregano on a sheet pan.
  2. Lay lemon slices down, set the salmon on top, and season.
  3. Roast 12 minutes until the salmon flakes.

Chicken Piccata

30 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

A thin dusting of flour browns the cutlets and thickens the pan sauce at the same time.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Season and flour the cutlets, sear 3 minutes per side, set aside.
  2. Add stock, lemon juice and capers; reduce by half.
  3. Swirl in butter, return chicken, spoon sauce over.

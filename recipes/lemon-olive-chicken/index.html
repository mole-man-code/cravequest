Lemon Olive Chicken

40 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Browning the skin first and finishing in the oven leaves the chicken crisp on top while the lemon and olives flavor the pan juices.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Sear the chicken skin side down in an oven-safe pan until deep golden, about 6 minutes; flip.
  2. Add the onion, garlic, lemon wedges, olives, thyme and stock around the chicken.
  3. Roast at 200°C for 20 minutes until cooked through and serve with the pan juices.

Fasolada

45 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Mashing a few beans into the broth thickens the soup without cream or flour.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Soften the onion, carrots and celery in olive oil for 8 minutes.
  2. Add the tomatoes, beans and stock and simmer 25 minutes, mashing some beans against the pot.
  3. Finish with parsley, lemon juice and a drizzle of olive oil.

Egg Fried Rice

15 minEggVegetarianEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Day-old rice is drier, so the grains fry and separate instead of clumping.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Scramble the eggs in a hot wok, set aside.
  2. Fry rice until it jumps and crackles.
  3. Return eggs, add peas, soy and sesame oil.

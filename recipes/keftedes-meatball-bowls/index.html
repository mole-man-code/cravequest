Keftedes Meatball Bowls

40 minBeef / PorkContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

A bread-and-milk paste keeps the meatballs tender, and grated onion adds moisture without chunks.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Mix the beef with the soaked bread, egg, onion, herbs and salt. Roll into small balls.
  2. Bake at 220°C for 15 minutes, turning once, until browned.
  3. Serve over rice with tzatziki.

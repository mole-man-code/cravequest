Chicken Shawarma Bowls

40 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

A yogurt and spice marinade tenderizes the chicken and helps it brown quickly under high heat.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken in yogurt, spices, garlic and lemon juice for at least 15 minutes.
  2. Roast at 230°C or sear in a hot pan until charred, about 15 minutes. Rest and slice.
  3. Serve over chopped cucumber and tomato with pita and a drizzle of tahini.

Thai Basil Chicken

20 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Very high heat and steady stirring give the ground chicken a caramelized crust before the basil wilts in at the end.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Pound the garlic and chilies to a rough paste. Mix the soy, oyster sauce and sugar.
  2. Fry the paste in hot oil 30 seconds, add the chicken, and cook until browned.
  3. Add the sauce, then the basil off the heat. Serve over rice.

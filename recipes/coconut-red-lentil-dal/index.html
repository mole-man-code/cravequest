Coconut Red Lentil Dal

30 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Red lentils fall apart quickly with no soaking, and a sizzling spiced oil poured on top adds a burst of flavor at the end.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Simmer the lentils with the turmeric, half the onion and the tomato for 20 minutes until soft.
  2. Mash lightly and season with salt.
  3. Heat the ghee, fry the cumin, remaining onion, garlic and chilies until golden, and pour it over the dal.

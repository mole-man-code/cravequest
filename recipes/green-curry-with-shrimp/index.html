Green Curry with Shrimp

30 minSeafoodContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Frying the paste in coconut cream first wakes up its aromatics before the liquid goes in.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Fry the paste in a spoon of coconut cream 1 minute.
  2. Add remaining milk and zucchini, simmer 8 minutes.
  3. Add shrimp until pink, finish with fish sauce, lime and basil.

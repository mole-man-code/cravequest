Skillet Margherita Pizza

25 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Starting the crust in a very hot skillet before it goes under the broiler gives a crisp bottom and blistered top without a pizza stone.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Heat the oven broiler to high and a heavy oven-safe skillet on the stove until very hot.
  2. Stretch the dough to fit the pan, oil the pan, and cook the dough on the stove 2 minutes until it puffs and browns underneath.
  3. Top with tomatoes and mozzarella, then broil 3 to 4 minutes until bubbling and spotted. Finish with basil.

Soft Tofu Stew

30 minTofuVegetarianEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Blooming the chili flakes in oil makes a deeply red, aromatic base for the delicate tofu.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Cook the garlic and gochugaru in sesame oil for 30 seconds.
  2. Add the stock, zucchini and mushrooms and simmer 8 minutes.
  3. Spoon in the tofu in big chunks, crack in the egg, and simmer 3 minutes. Top with scallions.

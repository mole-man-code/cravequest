Chicken Enchiladas

45 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Dipping the tortillas briefly in warm sauce softens them so they roll without cracking.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Mix the chicken with the onion and half the cheese.
  2. Warm the sauce, dip each tortilla in it, fill with chicken and roll into a baking dish.
  3. Pour over the remaining sauce, scatter with cheese, and bake 20 minutes until bubbling.

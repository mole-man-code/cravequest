Cacio e Pepe

20 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Finishing the pasta off the heat with starchy water keeps the cheese from clumping and turns it into a glossy sauce.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Toast cracked pepper in a dry pan 1 minute.
  2. Boil pasta in salted water, reserve 1 cup water, drain 2 minutes early.
  3. Finish pasta in the pepper pan with a ladle of water.
  4. Off the heat, toss in cheese with splashes of water until glossy.

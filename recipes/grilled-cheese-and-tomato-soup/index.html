Grilled Cheese and Tomato Soup

30 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Buttering the outside of the bread rather than the pan gives an evenly golden crust, and a spoonful of cream keeps the soup silky.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Soften the chopped onion in butter, add the tomatoes and stock, and simmer 15 minutes. Blend smooth and stir in the cream.
  2. Butter the bread, fill with cheddar, and toast in a skillet over medium heat 3 minutes per side.
  3. Serve the sandwiches for dipping.

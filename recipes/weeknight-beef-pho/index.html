Weeknight Beef Pho

40 minBeef / PorkContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Charring the onion and ginger first gives a fast broth the depth of a long-simmered one.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Char the onion and ginger under the broiler or in a dry pan until blackened in spots.
  2. Simmer with the stock, star anise, cinnamon and fish sauce for 25 minutes; strain.
  3. Pour the boiling broth over noodles and raw beef slices in bowls; add sprouts, basil and lime.

Dakgalbi

35 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Cooking the sweet potato and cabbage alongside the chicken in the same pan lets their juices thin the gochujang into a glossy sauce.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Mix the gochujang, soy sauce, sugar and garlic and coat the chicken. Rest 15 minutes.
  2. Cook the sweet potato and chicken in a wide pan over medium-high heat for 10 minutes.
  3. Add the cabbage and onion and cook 8 minutes more until everything is tender and coated. Serve with rice.

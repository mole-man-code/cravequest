Egg Curry

30 minEggVegetarianEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Frying the boiled eggs briefly first gives them a firm, browned skin that holds up in the sauce.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Boil the eggs 9 minutes, cool and peel. Fry them in oil until lightly blistered and set aside.
  2. Cook the onion until golden, add ginger, garlic and spices, then the tomatoes until they break down.
  3. Add a cup of water, simmer 8 minutes, add the eggs and finish with cilantro. Serve with rice.

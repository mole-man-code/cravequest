Shrimp and Grits

35 minSeafoodContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Finishing the grits with butter and cheese off the heat keeps them creamy, and searing the shrimp in a hot pan keeps them plump.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Simmer the grits in the water, stirring often, for 20 to 25 minutes until thick. Stir in half the butter and the cheese.
  2. Sear the shrimp in the remaining butter with garlic for 2 minutes per side.
  3. Spoon the grits into bowls, top with shrimp and scallions.

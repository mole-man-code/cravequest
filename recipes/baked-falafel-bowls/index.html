Baked Falafel Bowls

40 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Using canned chickpeas that have been thoroughly dried and only pulsed keeps the falafel crumbly-tender inside, and baking on a hot, oiled sheet crisps them without a fryer.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Dry the chickpeas well. Pulse with the onion, garlic, herbs, cumin, flour and salt until roughly chopped, not smooth.
  2. Shape into patties, place on an oiled sheet pan, and bake at 220°C for 25 minutes, flipping once.
  3. Serve over chopped cucumber and tomato with yogurt or tahini.

Yogurt-Marinated Chicken Thighs

40 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Yogurt's gentle acidity tenderizes the chicken, and broiling at high heat chars the edges in the way a tandoor would.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Mix the yogurt, garam masala, grated garlic and ginger, lemon juice and salt; coat the chicken and rest at least 15 minutes.
  2. Roast at 230°C or broil for 20 to 25 minutes until charred at the edges.
  3. Serve over rice with a squeeze of lemon.

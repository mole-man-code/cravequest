Lemongrass Chicken Banh Mi

40 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Quick-pickled carrots and cucumber cut through the rich chicken and add crunch.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken in lemongrass, fish sauce and sugar for at least 15 minutes.
  2. Sear or grill 6 minutes per side until charred. Slice.
  3. Fill toasted baguettes with mayo, chicken, pickles, cucumber and cilantro.

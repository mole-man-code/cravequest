Aloo Gobi

35 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Browning the cauliflower and potato first in a wide pan gives them caramelized edges that carry the spices.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Brown the potato and cauliflower in oil in batches; set aside.
  2. Sizzle the cumin seeds, then cook the onion, ginger and garlic until soft. Add the tomatoes and turmeric.
  3. Return the vegetables, add a splash of water, cover, and cook 10 minutes until tender. Finish with cilantro.

Rolled Korean Omelet

15 minEggVegetarianEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Cooking the egg in thin layers and rolling as you go builds the omelet up in tender, even spirals.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Whisk the eggs with the scallions, carrot and salt.
  2. Pour a thin layer into an oiled nonstick pan over medium-low heat. When almost set, roll it to one side.
  3. Pour in another thin layer, lifting the roll so egg runs underneath, and roll again. Repeat, then slice.

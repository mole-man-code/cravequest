Miso Salmon Rice Bowl

20 minSeafoodContains meat or fishEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Miso's sugars caramelize fast under high heat, so a two-ingredient rub does the work of a long marinade.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Rub salmon with miso and mirin.
  2. Roast at 220°C for 10 minutes.
  3. Serve over rice with sliced cucumber and sesame.

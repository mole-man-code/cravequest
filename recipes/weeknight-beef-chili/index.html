Weeknight Beef Chili

45 minBeef / PorkContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Blooming the chili powder and cumin in the fat before adding liquid deepens the flavor of the whole pot.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Brown the beef with the onion; drain excess fat.
  2. Stir in garlic, chili powder and cumin for 1 minute.
  3. Add tomatoes, beans and stock; simmer 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.

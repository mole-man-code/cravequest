Palak Paneer

35 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Blanching the spinach and blending it briefly keeps the sauce bright green instead of muddy.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Blanch the spinach 1 minute, cool in cold water, then blend with the chilies.
  2. Sizzle the cumin seeds, then cook the onion, ginger and garlic until soft.
  3. Add the spinach purée and garam masala, simmer 5 minutes, then stir in the paneer and cream.

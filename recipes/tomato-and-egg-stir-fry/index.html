Tomato and Egg Stir-Fry

15 minEggVegetarianEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Cooking the eggs until barely set and returning them at the end keeps them soft instead of rubbery.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Scramble the eggs in hot oil until just set and remove them.
  2. Cook the tomatoes with the sugar and salt until they break down into a sauce.
  3. Fold the eggs back in with the scallions and serve over rice.

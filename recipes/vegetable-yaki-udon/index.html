Vegetable Yaki Udon

20 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Loosening the noodles in hot water first keeps them from clumping and breaking apart in the pan.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Rinse the udon under hot water to loosen the strands.
  2. Stir-fry the carrot, mushrooms and cabbage over high heat for 4 minutes.
  3. Add the noodles, soy sauce and mirin and toss for 2 minutes. Finish with sesame oil and scallions.

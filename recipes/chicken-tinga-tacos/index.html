Chicken Tinga Tacos

35 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Poaching thighs first keeps them juicy, and the smoky chipotle sauce clings to the shredded meat.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Simmer chicken in water with half the onion 20 minutes, shred.
  2. Blend tomatoes, chipotles and remaining onion; simmer 10 minutes.
  3. Fold in chicken, serve in warm tortillas with lime.

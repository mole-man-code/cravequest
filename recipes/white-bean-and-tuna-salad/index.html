White Bean and Tuna Salad

10 minSeafoodContains meat or fishEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Dressing the beans while they are still slightly warm helps them absorb the lemon and oil.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Rinse the beans, and soak the sliced onion in cold water for 5 minutes to mellow it.
  2. Whisk lemon juice, olive oil and salt and toss with the beans.
  3. Fold in the tuna, onion, capers and parsley.

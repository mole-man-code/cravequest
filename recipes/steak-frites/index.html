Steak Frites

40 minBeef / PorkContains meat or fishEffort 3 of 3

Why this recipe works

Resting the steak and building a pan sauce from the browned bits captures flavor that would otherwise be lost.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Toss the fries with oil and roast at 230°C for 30 minutes, turning once.
  2. Sear the seasoned steaks 3 to 4 minutes per side with butter and thyme. Rest 5 minutes.
  3. Cook the shallot in the pan, add the wine, reduce by half, and swirl in butter to finish.

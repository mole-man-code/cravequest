Baked Feta Pasta

35 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Roasting the tomatoes with the feta turns them into a jammy sauce that you simply stir together.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Put the tomatoes, whole garlic cloves and feta in a baking dish and coat with olive oil.
  2. Roast 25 minutes until the tomatoes burst and the feta is soft and browned.
  3. Cook the pasta, then stir it into the dish, mashing the feta and garlic into a sauce. Top with basil.

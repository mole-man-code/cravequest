Egg Drop Soup

15 minEggContains meat or fishEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Pouring the beaten egg in a slow stream while stirring gently makes silky ribbons instead of clumps.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Simmer the stock with sliced ginger for 5 minutes. Stir in the soy sauce.
  2. Whisk the cornstarch with 2 tbsp water and stir in until the soup thickens slightly.
  3. Swirl the soup and pour in the egg in a thin stream. Wait 10 seconds, stir gently, and top with scallions.

Bulgogi Beef Bowls

35 minBeef / PorkContains meat or fishEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Grated pear in the marinade tenderizes the thin-sliced beef and adds a light sweetness.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Mix the pear, soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil and garlic; marinate the beef at least 20 minutes.
  2. Sear the beef in a very hot pan in small batches so it browns instead of steaming.
  3. Serve over rice with scallions.

Smash Burgers

20 minBeef / PorkContains meat or fishEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Smashing loosely packed beef onto a very hot pan maximizes browned, crispy surface area.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Roll beef into 4 loose balls.
  2. Smash hard onto a screaming-hot pan, season, cook 2 minutes.
  3. Flip, add cheese, stack on toasted buns.

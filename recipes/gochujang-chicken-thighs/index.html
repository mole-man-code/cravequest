Gochujang Chicken Thighs

35 minChickenContains meat or fishEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Gochujang's sugars caramelize in the oven, giving a sticky glaze without any frying.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Whisk the gochujang, soy sauce, honey, sesame oil and garlic; coat the chicken.
  2. Roast at 220°C for 25 minutes, basting once, until charred at the edges.
  3. Scatter with sesame seeds and serve with rice.

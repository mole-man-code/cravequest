Minestrone

40 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 1 of 3

Why this recipe works

Adding the pasta and beans near the end keeps them from turning mushy, and a Parmesan rind or a handful of cheese at the table adds savory depth.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Soften the onion, carrots and celery in olive oil for 8 minutes.
  2. Add the tomatoes, stock and zucchini and simmer 15 minutes.
  3. Add the beans and pasta and cook until the pasta is tender, about 8 minutes. Serve with Parmesan.

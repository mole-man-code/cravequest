Ratatouille

50 minVegetablesVegetarianEffort 2 of 3

Why this recipe works

Cooking each vegetable separately before combining them keeps distinct textures instead of one uniform mush.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Cut everything into similar chunks.
  2. Sauté the eggplant, zucchini and pepper in batches in olive oil until golden; set aside.
  3. Cook the onion and garlic, add the tomatoes and thyme, then return all the vegetables and simmer 15 minutes.
